The specialty

Two signature techniques

Digital perms and Japanese and Korean magic straightening are hands on services. Here is how each one goes, step by step.

Digital Perm

A Korean style digital perm builds soft, long lasting curl and body using heated rods and a multi step process.

  1. 1Consultation on curl size, pattern, and how much body you want
  2. 2Heated rod processing, timed to your hair's length and texture
  3. 3Cool down set, rinse, and a finished style

Magic Straightening

Japanese and Korean magic straightening smooths and relaxes hair for a frizz free, glossy finish.

  1. 1Consultation on your natural texture and the finish you're after
  2. 2Straightening treatment applied and heat processed in sections
  3. 3Finish style with a shine sealing step

Since 2000

A Convoy Street fixture, one appointment at a time

Sunny Day People Hair Salon has been part of the Convoy District since 2000, offering Korean style digital perms, Japanese and Korean magic straightening, color, and precision cuts for men and women. Beyond hair, a visit can round out with updos for special occasions, makeup, Korean skin care, and nails. It has grown into one of the busiest salons on Convoy Street, with more than a thousand reviews and thousands of photos shared by guests on Yelp.
